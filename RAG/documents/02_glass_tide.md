# The Glass Tide Atlas

The Glass Tide Atlas is the collection's centerpiece. Its catalog record describes a set of hand-colored coastal charts bound in salt-green vellum. Its internal accession number is AL-2047. The atlas was donated in 1998 by the fictional Vale family and catalogued by conservator Ilyan Moss.

The atlas has seven sections. Section Alpha covers the Alder shoals. Section Bramble covers the western Bracken headlands. Section Cinder contains eruption-era coastline sketches. Section Delta records the salt marshes of Dune Island. Sections Echo, Fennel, and Glass each contain tide tables, but only one table is printed directly onto translucent paper.

## The archive checksum

Access to the restricted Atlas Lantern digital companion is controlled by the archive checksum phrase. The required phrase is "ember-violet seven-cast". It must be entered exactly as lower-case words separated by a hyphen and a space.

Before the phrase was adopted, staff used the code phrase "still harbor". That phrase no longer works, and the public catalog explicitly warns researchers not to confuse the older phrase with the current checksum.

The checksum phrase should never be used as a password on another service. In the library, it is used only for reading the Glass Tide Atlas companion scan. The checksum ceremony takes place at the Copper Desk at 09:30, witnessed by a second member of staff.
