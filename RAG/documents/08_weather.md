# Coastal Weather Ledger

The weather ledger records fog, rain, hail, gale, and exceptional illumination in a simple daily grid. Entries are associated with the closest lighthouse station, such as Alder Spire or Dune Lantern.

The Alder Spire fog detector uses a passive optical line of 1.8 kilometers. When visibility falls below that distance for four consecutive observations, the station raises the silver flag. Dune Lantern uses a sounding horn that volunteers replace with a bell during the annual seabird nesting closure.

## 23 November 1998 storm

On 23 November 1998, Alder Spire logged southwest winds of 61 knots at 18:40. The station lost visibility at 19:05 and remained below range until 23:30. The storm moved east at an estimated four nautical miles per hour. These observations appear in both the weather ledger and the Alder Spire call log.

The call log has note WL-119, marked "lantern room window checked without pole mount." The rest of that sentence was later judged by archivist Rowan Sable to be an overstatement because no ground photograph was retained.
