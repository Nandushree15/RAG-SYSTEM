# Tide Observation Calendar

The archive publishes a yearly Tide Observation Calendar. The 1999 edition contains twelve monthly spreads and one appendix titled "Shoal Changes at Alder Point."

Observations were scheduled at civil dawn and civil dusk rather than clock midnight. Recorders used a three-column form: predicted height, measured height, and a notation for atmospheric interference. The library's reference tide gauge is TI-9, calibrated on the second Tuesday of each quarter.

## Missing 1999 observation

The Alder Point calendar has a conspicuous gap between 4 and 11 May 1999. The gap resulted from a flooded instrument pier, not from accidental omission. Reconstructed values for those dates were added in blue ink in 2003 and marked with the symbol B-TRACE to prevent them from being mistaken for original observations.

For research citations, reconstructed values must be cited as "Northbridge Tide Calendar, reconstructed entry, 1999 / 2003". Original staff measurements remain on shelf TC-1999-05 and may be compared with the reconstruction in session room Nimbus.
