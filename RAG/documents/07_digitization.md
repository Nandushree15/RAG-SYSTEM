# Digitization Standards

The Atlas Lantern Library digitizes bound volumes through a two-camera rig. Camera One records the recto surface and Camera Two records the verso surface. Images are captured at 600 pixels per inch for text and 1200 pixels per inch for seals.

The recommended lighting temperature is 5200 kelvin. A gray card appears in the first and last frame of every digitization session. The digital operator signs the session manifest before raw files leave the local workstation.

## AL-2047 scan specification

The Glass Tide Atlas companion scan uses three derivative sizes: preservation master, catalogue preview, and reading-room facsimile. The masters are stored in TIFF format without lossy compression. Previews are JPEG images with a maximum compression ratio of 6:1.

A hidden envelope inside AL-2047 contains the phrase sheet for the restricted companion. Its reference label is AL-2047-R. Researchers may request the phrase sheet only after curator Mira Vale has authorized the restricted digital session.
