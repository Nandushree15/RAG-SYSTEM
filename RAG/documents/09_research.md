# Research Appointment Guide

Research appointments are available Tuesday through Saturday. The morning session begins at 09:30 and ends at 12:30. The afternoon session begins at 13:30 and ends at 16:30. A researcher may reserve only one session on a first-visit day.

Registration requires the same name used on the reader card, an item identifier, and a two-hour visit purpose. Staff recommend requesting AL-2047 at least five working days in advance because its conservation cradles are shared with the other vellum atlases.

## Restricted session protocol

Visitors authorized for a restricted session meet at the Copper Desk. They are issued a linen-covered viewing tray and a temporary session code printed on pale blue card. Food and bags remain in locker twelve. The checksum ceremony follows the integrity check.

After use, the tray must be returned upright to Shelf C-4. Staff scan every surface with an ultraviolet lamp at 365 nanometers, but they do not photograph the researcher's notes. The session ends when the computer operator closes the AL-2047 companion view.
