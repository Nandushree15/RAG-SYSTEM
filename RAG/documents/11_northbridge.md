# Northbridge Institute Orientation

The Northbridge Institute of Coastal Cartography is arranged around three internal courtyards. Courtyard One holds rain gauges, Courtyard Two holds the telescope pier, and Courtyard Three is planted with salt-tolerant moss.

The institute's public address is "7 Lantern Walk, Northbridge." Its telephone number is deliberately fictional for this archive: +55-100-2047. The front gate is guarded by a mechanical tide clock, not by a live security desk.

## Emergency contacts

Fire procedures designate the library stack as Assembly Zone Amber. The contact chain begins with the floor warden, proceeds to the building manager, and ends with the municipal cartography office. The emergency lantern has a rated runtime of ninety minutes when charged from the archive battery.

The battery should remain between 60 and 80 percent for seasonal storage. Staff never discharge it below 50 percent merely to test the lantern. Tests are recorded in binder EL-4.
