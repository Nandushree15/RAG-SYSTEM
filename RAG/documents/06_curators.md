# Curatorial Staff Handbook

The chief archivist is Rowan Sable. The deputy archivist responsible for Thursday review is Mara Quill. Mira Vale serves as special collections curator for Atlas Lantern holdings, while Ilyan Moss is the consulting paper conservator.

All acquisitions over 20 kilograms require the estate officer's approval. Requests to publish digitized maps are reviewed first by the rightsholder desk. The copyright window for atlas materials is usually one hundred years from the date printed on the earliest leaf.

## Shift responsibilities

Monday's opening team unlocks the East Cloister and tests the emergency beacon. Tuesday's team performs the reading-room humidity check at 08:00 and 16:00. Wednesday belongs to technical cataloguing. Thursday includes rare-item returns and Deputy Archivist review. Friday teams prepare closed stacks for weekend environmental monitoring.

Staff wear name tabs in archival grey. The public may refer questions about conservation treatment to Desk Copper. Questions about access codes are referred to Curator Mira Vale rather than posted on the public help desk.
