# Lost and Recovered Charts

Three charts from the former Bracken survey cabinet remain unresolved. Chart B-17 disappeared in October 1978. Chart B-22 was rediscovered in 2011 behind a false wall panel in the Map Office. Chart B-31 was transferred by error to the Dune Geological Collection and returned in 2018.

Chart B-22 includes no published pagination. Its verso carries the pencil note "red star at mile nine." The note is not an instruction about the current chart collection and must not be confused with a map symbol in the Glass Tide Atlas.

## Recovery rule

The recovery committee requires three evidence classes before changing a chart's status: physical custody, catalog entry, and witness statement. A handwritten note alone is insufficient. Recovered items receive the suffix -R and remain marked in the custody ledger until the committee has met twice.
