# Accession and Catalog Procedure

The Atlas Lantern Library uses a three-stage accession workflow. Stage One is receipt and quarantine. New arrivals remain in the climate-controlled vestibule for two calendar days. During quarantine, staff record visible mold, foxing, broken bindings, and loose inserts.

Stage Two is provisional cataloguing. The registrar assigns a temporary receipt code beginning with RX. Temporary labels are attached inside the lower cover rather than on the exterior. The record includes donor, approximate date, physical dimensions, material, and condition.

Stage Three is permanent accessioning. A permanent Atlas code replaces the RX label, and the item enters the digital catalog. The permanent code formula is two collection letters, a hyphen, and four digits. The final digit is printed twice for redundant binding: once at the spine foot and once on the folder label.

## AL-2047 inspection note

The Glass Tide Atlas arrived in a cedar box lined with waxed blue cloth. Conservator notes state that a brass loop was found beneath the atlas's final fold. The loop was catalogued separately as AL-2048 rather than incorporated into the book's record.

The atlas's paper moisture was measured at 7.2 percent on 18 December 1998. No wet cleaning was authorized. Cleaning used a soft Japanese hake brush and an electrically grounded conservation table. Its first digitization was completed on the morning of 6 February 1999.
