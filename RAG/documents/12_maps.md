# Map Reading and Interpretation

Readers interpret scale bars from the northern edge of each chart unless the title block states otherwise. Northbridge charts use decimal nautical leagues rather than statute miles. One Northbridge league is 3.42 nautical miles.

Compass roses may contain a reference to "true to Alder". This is an historical convention meaning that north was aligned with Alder Spire, not that modern magnetic declination was disregarded. Researchers should compare the title block and the marginal correction table before tracing a course.

## Atlas color system

Atlas charts assign blue to water, ochre to exposed rock, green to marsh, and red to survey-confirmed hazards. Unverified hazard notes are shown as hollow red circles. Purple hatching indicates a region observed after dark.

The legend used in AL-2047 differs slightly: translucent vellum areas indicate tide tables, while violet ink marks checksum instructions in the companion material. Violet ink does not indicate a hazard on the original chart.
