# Atlas Lantern Library: Foundation Guide

The Atlas Lantern Library is a fictional research archive operated by the fictional Northbridge Institute of Coastal Cartography. It opened its public reading room on 14 March 1986. The library exists to preserve route surveys, lighthouse observations, tide records, and navigation folklore from four fictional island regions: Alder, Bracken, Cinder, and Dune.

Every collection item receives an Atlas identifier. The prefix identifies the collection, while a four-digit accession number identifies the item itself. For example, AL-2047 belongs to the Atlas Lantern collection and is accession 2047.

The reading room is arranged in twelve oak bays. Bay Seven contains small-format charts, Bay Nine contains instrument repair manuals, and Bay Eleven contains oral histories. Researchers enter through the East Cloister and must leave rolled maps in the drying cabinet before crossing the reading-room threshold.

## Reading-room rules

Reader cards are issued from Desk Aurora. A visitor selecting more than five fragile volumes signs the violet register. The violet register is reviewed every Thursday by the deputy archivist. Pens are prohibited because the library's calcium-based survey sheets can be marked permanently by ink solvent.

Borrowing is not permitted for Atlas items marked with a copper tag. Copper-tagged items may be inspected at one of four conservation tables. Each table includes an adjustable lamp rated for 400 lux, which is bright enough for detailed transcription without exposing pigments to unnecessary light.
